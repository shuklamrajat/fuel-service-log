## Full description

```text
See what your car really costs, and never miss an oil change. Wrenchnote is a fuel log, MPG tracker and car maintenance reminder in one friendly app, built to give you numbers you can actually trust.

Log a fill-up in seconds and Wrenchnote works out your real fuel economy, tank by tank. Add your services and it reminds you when the next one is due, by date, by distance or both. No account, no ads, and your car's history stays on your phone.

✨ HOW IT WORKS
1. Add your car: a name and, if you like, make, model and year
2. Each time you fill up, log the odometer, the fuel and what you paid
3. Watch your fuel economy, costs and service reminders build up on their own

⛽ FUEL ECONOMY YOU CAN TRUST
• Log fill-ups in seconds: date, odometer, fuel and total paid
• Partial fill-ups are carried into the next full tank, so your numbers stay right
• Missed a fill-up? Mark it, and that tank is left out instead of skewing your average
• Add an old fill-up later and the history recalculates
• Odometer checks catch typos before they're saved
• See your average economy, your last tank and a chart of every tank over time

🧮 WHY THE NUMBERS ARE RIGHT
Fuel economy is only accurate measured from one full tank to the next. Wrenchnote uses this full-to-full method automatically, folding partial top-ups into the next full tank and skipping any tank with a missed fill-up, so one forgotten receipt never ruins your average.

📏 YOUR UNITS, YOUR WAY
• Fuel economy in L/100 km, km/L, US mpg or UK mpg
• Kilometres or miles, litres or gallons
• Switch any time: your history is stored once and shown in the units you choose

🔧 SERVICE REMINDERS
• Remind by time, distance or both: oil every 6 months or 5,000 km, whichever comes first
• A ring on each reminder shows how much of the interval is used
• See at a glance what's overdue and what's due soon
• Log a service and its reminder resets
• Keep a service history with dates, odometer, cost and notes

💸 EVERY COST IN ONE PLACE
• Total running cost, split between fuel and service
• A monthly chart of the last six months of spending
• Distance driven, so the cost per km or mile is based on real driving

🛠️ MEET WRENCHNOTE
Wrenchnote is a little logbook with a wrench and a smile, and it's designed to feel good to use: smooth animations, charts that draw themselves as your data grows, and a calm dark mode for late drives. Prefer less movement? Wrenchnote follows your phone's reduce-motion setting.

🚗 EVERY VEHICLE, AND EXPORTS (PRO)
• Track as many cars, motorbikes, vans and trucks as you own, each with its own history
• Export the full history as CSV for spreadsheets
• Export a PDF history, handy when you sell the car and want to show it was looked after

🔒 PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
• No account and no sign-up
• No ads and no tracking
• Your logs are stored on your phone and never uploaded by us
• Exports only go where you choose to send them

❓ GOOD TO KNOW
• Works offline: log a fill-up at the pump with no signal
• Pick your currency in Settings
• Light or dark theme, or follow your phone

WHO IT'S FOR
Drivers who want to know their real fuel consumption and keep on top of maintenance: daily commuters, families with more than one car, rideshare and delivery drivers tracking costs, motorbike riders, and anyone getting a car ready to sell with a full service record.

FREE AND PRO
Wrenchnote is free for one vehicle with unlimited fill-ups, reminders, services and cost reports. Wrenchnote Pro adds more vehicles and exports: $19.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $39.99 once for life. Prices may vary by country and are shown in the app before you buy.

Questions or ideas? We read every message, and the best ideas end up in the app.
```
